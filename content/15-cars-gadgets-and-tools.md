---
label: Chapter 15
title: The Laws of Cars, Gadgets and Tools
---

15:1 — He who borrows a tool shall return it, clean, within one week. Otherwise it becomes his, and the lender's anger becomes tradition.

15:2 — The video tutorial that says "this takes ten minutes" lies. It takes three hours, two trips to the hardware store, and a plumber.

15:3 — Read the manual. Or at least look at the pictures. The instructions were written by a man who has already suffered for thee.

15:4 — Measure twice, cut once. Then measure a third time, because thy first two measurements were optimistic.

15:5 — Every man shall know how to change a tyre, jump a battery, check the oil, hang a shelf, reset a breaker, and tie a tie. Hand these skills down.

15:6 — Know thy limits. Gas, electricity and anything that begins with "load-bearing" belong to a licensed professional. Pride is expensive. So is the fire brigade.

15:7 — The new phone, the new watch, the new console: the joy fades within a month. Buy fewer things. Use them longer. Repair them if thou canst.

15:8 — Thy car is not thy personality. It is a metal box that gets thee to work. Maintain it, insure it, and drive it as if every other driver were thy brother's wife.

15:9 — Never drive after drinking. Not after one, not after "just a couple." A taxi costs less than a funeral, a lawsuit or a lifetime of guilt.

15:10 — Passwords are not "password123." Turn on two-factor, back up thy photos, and update thy phone. Every man who ignored this has a story.

15:11 — Whoever brings the right tool at the right moment is a hero. Keep a small kit in thy car and thy home. Be the bro with the screwdriver.
