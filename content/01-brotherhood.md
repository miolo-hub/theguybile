---
label: Chapter 1
title: The Laws of Brotherhood
---

1:1 — A bro shall never let another bro walk alone when the situation is questionable.

1:2 — What happens on boys' night stays on boys' night, unless it is too funny not to tell.

1:3 — A bro's enemy is not automatically another bro's enemy, but he shall be investigated.

1:4 — Thou shalt answer the phone when a brother calls at 2 a.m. It is either an emergency, a breakup, or a life-changing business idea. All three deserve your ears.

1:5 — "I'm fine" is the most common lie among men. When a brother says it twice, show up with snacks and say nothing clever.

1:6 — Celebrate your brother's win louder than your own. His promotion is not your demotion.

1:7 — Tell your brother the truth when the food is in his teeth, the girlfriend is wrong for him, or the business plan is a pyramid. Flattery is a cheap friend. Honesty is expensive and worth it.

1:8 — Roast with love, never with the wound. Everyone knows where the bruise is. A true bro walks around it.

1:9 — When thy brother moves, help him. When thy brother moves into a fourth-floor walk-up, help him, then ask yourself why you said yes.

1:10 — Check on the quiet one. The loud brothers are loud because they get noticed. The quiet one is carrying something heavy and hoping you'll ask.

1:11 — Friendship is a garden, not a vending machine. If you only appear when you need something, you are a pigeon, not a bro.

1:12 — Thou shalt not ghost a brother. Reply, even if it is just "swamped, will call Sunday." Then actually call Sunday.
