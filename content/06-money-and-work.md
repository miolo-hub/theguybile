---
label: Chapter 6
title: The Laws of Money and Work
---

6:1 — Pay thyself first. Even 10 percent. The man who saves nothing is one car repair away from a panic attack.

6:2 — Build an emergency fund of three to six months of expenses. It is not boring. It is the price of saying "no" to a bad boss.

6:3 — Thou shalt not lend a brother money that thou canst not afford to never see again. Lend it as a gift, and be pleasantly surprised if it returns.

6:4 — The car, the watch and the apartment are not thy worth. Many a man is rich on paper and miserable in a very nice kitchen.

6:5 — Learn how credit cards work before they work thee. Interest is a quiet monster.

6:6 — A job is a trade: your time for their money. Make sure you are not selling thy whole life at a discount.

6:7 — Ask for the raise. The worst they say is "no," and you were already at "no."

6:8 — Thy boss is a human being with a boss. Be kind, be useful, be honest, and do not bring the rage-email to life.

6:9 — When thou art laid off, it is not a verdict on thy worth. It is a spreadsheet decision made by people who have never met thy dog.

6:10 — Do not marry thy career. It will not attend thy funeral.

6:11 — Talk about money openly with thy friends and thy partner. Silence about it is why men fear it. Shame feeds on silence.

6:12 — If it sounds too good to be true, and a bro on social media is selling a course about it, it is a pyramid with a ring light.

*Note: the Guybile is not financial advice. It is bro advice. Talk to a real adviser for real decisions.*
