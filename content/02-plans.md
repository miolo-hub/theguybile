label: Chapter 2
title: The Laws of Plans
---

69:69 — That's why no trip planned goes as planned.

2:1 — The more excited the group is about a trip, the higher the probability of cancellation.

2:2 — One bro saying "I'm 5 minutes away" shall be interpreted as 45 minutes.

2:3 — A plan without a backup plan is merely a dream.

2:4 — The group chat that says "we should totally do this" has made a plan roughly 4 percent of the time. Someone must book something. Be that someone.

2:5 — If nobody picks the restaurant, the restaurant will be picked by hunger and rage at 9:40 p.m., and it will be a gas station.

2:6 — Whoever says "I'll handle the tickets" and does not handle the tickets shall be known henceforth as the Ticket Man. His name shall be spoken with sighs.

2:7 — Set a budget before the trip, not after. Money talks get worse at the hotel bar.

2:8 — Five years from now, no one will remember the schedule. They will remember the flat tire, the wrong turn, and the story that begins with "remember when Dave..." Plan for the chaos, because the chaos is the souvenir.

2:9 — Life plans fail too. Thou shalt have a five-year plan and the humility to burn it at year two. The man who adapts outlives the man who is merely certain.
