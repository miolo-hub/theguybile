label: Chapter 9
title: The Laws of the Body
---

9:1 — Sleep seven hours. Not as a luxury, but as a performance-enhancing drug that is legal and free.

9:2 — Move thy body most days. Lift something, walk somewhere, chase something. The body is built for motion and complains in silence until it complains in pain.

9:3 — See a doctor. Yes, even for that thing. Men are less likely than women to go for checkups, and then wonder why the news arrives late.

9:4 — A lump, a mole that changes, blood where there should be none, pain that lingers for weeks: these are not things to "tough out." Call this week, not next year.

9:5 — Thy gym is not a courtroom. No one is judging the weights. Everyone is thinking about their own bench.

9:6 — Protein is good. Creatine is fine. Seventeen supplements from a man with a podcast are thy money, wasted.

9:7 — Wear sunscreen. Thy forehead will thank thee at fifty.

9:8 — Brush, floss, hydrate. The three sacred rites of the adult man. None of them are glamorous. All of them are cheaper than a root canal.

9:9 — Thy hairline is not thy worth. If it goes, let it go, or embrace the shave. The bald man with confidence beats the comb-over with fear.

9:10 — Every body is different. Thy goal is not to look like a man in an advertisement who has a photographer, a chef and a lighting technician. It is to feel strong, move well, and live long.

9:11 — Sexual health is health. Use protection, get tested, and speak honestly with thy partner and thy doctor. If something does not work, it is common, it is treatable, and no one will laugh at a doctor's office.

9:12 — Wash thy hands, thy sheets and thy water bottle. Hygiene is a love language that everyone speaks.

### Of the Gym, and Why a Brother Goeth

9:13 — Lift, for after thirty a man loseth a little muscle every year he doth not use it. Strength is not vanity. It is the ability to carry thy groceries, thy children and thy friend's sofa up three flights at sixty.

9:14 — Train thy body and thy mind followeth. Exercise lifteth the mood, easeth worry and sharpeneth thought. Many a brother hath walked into the gym with a problem and walked out with a plan.

9:15 — The barbell is the cheapest therapist in town. It listeneth, it never interrupteth, and it is always honest about how much thou canst carry. But if the weight on thy mind will not lift, see a real one too.

9:16 — Strong legs, strong heart, strong bones. Squat, walk, climb the stairs. Cardio is not punishment for pizza. It is rent paid on the only house thou wilt live in forever.

9:17 — Discipline built at 6 a.m. in an empty gym followeth thee to the office, the exam and the hard conversation. The rep thou didst not want to do is the one that built thee.

9:18 — Start with what thou canst do, not what thou sawest on the internet. Three days a week, done for a year, beateth seven days a week, done for a fortnight.

9:19 — Form before ego. Lower the weight and keep thy spine. No brother ever impressed anyone from a physiotherapist's table.

9:20 — Eat enough protein, drink enough water, and sleep. The gym breaketh the muscle; the kitchen and the bed rebuild it.

9:21 — Bring a brother. A spotter catcheth the bar, a gym partner catcheth thee on the days thou wouldst have stayed in bed.

9:22 — A sound mind in a strong body. Read a book after the workout. The man who trains only his body becometh a wall, and the man who trains only his mind becometh a ghost. Be a temple: walls and a soul.
