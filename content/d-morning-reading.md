title: The Morning Reading
---

Seven mornings. Seven short stories. One small mission each. No lectures, no gurus, no cold showers required.

### Monday: The Man Who Started on Mondays

Arjun had been "starting Monday" for four years. His gym bag had seen more of his car's boot than any treadmill. Then one Tuesday, a day no one trusts, he did ten push-ups in his socks, in the dark, before anyone could watch him fail. He has not missed a Tuesday since. Monday is a promise. Tuesday is the proof. **Today's mission:** do ten of anything, right now, before thy phone realises thou art awake.

### Tuesday: The Group Chat Heist

Four brothers planned a trip for three years. The chat held fourteen thousand messages and zero tickets. Then Dev, who had said nothing all year, booked all four seats at 2 a.m. and sent one line: "Dates are final." Fourteen thousand opinions bowed before one payment. Legends are not the loudest voice in the chat. They are the one who presses **Pay**. **Today's mission:** do the thing the group chat keeps "planning". Book it, send it, or fix a date.

### Wednesday: The Interview That Went Wrong

Vikram spilled coffee over his own CV, called the interviewer by the wrong name, and then sneezed during the salary question. He walked out sure his career was over. The company called on Friday. The interviewer said, "Anyone who can sneeze through a salary question can survive our Mondays." Embarrassment is a two-minute event in the world and a two-year event in thy head. **Today's mission:** do one thing that scares thee by five per cent. Ask, apply, say hello first.

### Thursday: The Jar

Sam put the price of one chai in a jar every day. His brothers laughed. "That is not a plan, that is pocket change." Two years later the jar bought a flight to the sea. The same brothers carried his bags and called it "supporting the vision". Small money, moved daily, beats big money, planned someday. **Today's mission:** set aside the cost of one chai, now. Name the jar after something thou wantest.

### Friday: The Call

Karan had not called his oldest friend in two years. Every week the silence grew heavier, until it felt too late to say anything. Finally he called and said, "This is awkward, so I am going to be brave." There was a pause. Then: "Do not hang up. I have been trying to call thee for a month." Both were waiting. Both were afraid. One of them dialled. **Today's mission:** call, not text, one brother thou hast lost touch with.

### Saturday: The Brother Who Learned One Dish

Ravi could not cook. At a party, he decided to learn one dish and perfect it. Twenty minutes of practice, three weekends in a row. The fourth weekend he hosted, and the whole flat stood around his biryani in silence. Every party since, the brothers ask, "Ravi, what are we eating?" He did not become a chef. He became a man who can be trusted with a kitchen, and that is better. **Today's mission:** spend twenty minutes learning one skill thou hast been avoiding. Cook it, fix it, play it, write it.

### Sunday: The Reckoning

At the end of the week, Dev counted his wins, however small: ten push-ups, one call, one chai in a jar, one awkward hello. It looked like nothing. Then he looked at the same week from last year, and it had been a blank. The man who counts nothing thinks he did nothing. The man who counts a little sees that he is moving. **Today's mission:** write down three wins from this week, and send a thank-you to one person who helped thee win them.

### Then repeat

Mornings are free. Thou mayest begin again every single one of them.

*Rise.*

*Do the small thing.*

*Tell a brother.*
