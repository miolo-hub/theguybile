In the beginning there was a group chat, and the group chat was without form, and void, and had 4,000 unread messages.

The Guybile exists because nobody gave men a manual. Boys were handed a football, a lecture about "being a man," and a hard stare when they cried. This book is the manual we should have gotten: funny enough to read in the bathroom, honest enough to change something.

**How to read it.** Every verse is chapter:verse, like scripture. Some verses are jokes. Some are jokes that stop being funny halfway through. Some are plain advice wearing a joke as a hat. Read it with your friends, argue about it, and add your own verses.

**The three foundations.**

- **A man can be strong and soft in the same hour.** Lifting heavy things and talking about feelings are not enemies.
- **A man's worth is not his salary, his body count, or his bench press.** It is whether people are safer, calmer and better off because he walked in.
- **Nobody makes it alone.** The lone wolf mostly ends up as a lone guy eating cereal at 2 a.m., wondering why nobody called.

This is the Guybile. Go forth, be useful, and text your friend back.
