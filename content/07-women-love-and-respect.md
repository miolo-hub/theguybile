---
label: Chapter 7
title: The Laws of Women, Love and Respect
---

Women are not a mystery to be solved, a prize to be won, or a species to be decoded. They are people, with the same fears, jokes, bills and bad days as you. Most of the "mystery" disappears when you listen.

7:1 — Treat every woman as thou wouldst want thy sister, thy mother and thy daughter to be treated. Then go one step further and treat her like a person with her own mind, because she is not only someone's sister.

7:2 — Listen without fixing. When she says "my day was awful," the correct reply is rarely a seven-step plan. Try "that sounds awful. Want to tell me more?"

7:3 — When she says "no," or "maybe not," or goes quiet, or pulls away, that is a no. Consent is not a hurdle. It is a conversation, and it must be enthusiastic, clear and ongoing. Silence is never a yes.

7:4 — Rejection is not an insult. It is information. Say "okay, take care," and mean it. The man who takes a no with grace is the man people remember kindly.

7:5 — Say what thou meanest. Games, hints and "I'll just see if she texts first" are not romance. They are fear wearing cologne.

7:6 — Thou shalt not ghost. If you are not feeling it, say so kindly and clearly. A short honest message beats a long disappearing act.

7:7 — Do thy share. In the home, with the kids, with the dishes, with the mental load of remembering the dentist, the birthday and the thing in the fridge. If she is the only one who "notices," you are not helping. You are a guest.

7:8 — A woman is not a trophy, a conquest or a scoreboard. The man who counts is not winning anything.

7:9 — Thy female friends, colleagues and bosses are not here for thy approval or thy flirting. Collaborate with them. Learn from them. You will be amazed how much you missed.

7:10 — Never talk about a woman among friends in a way thou wouldst not say in front of her. The locker room is also a mirror.

7:11 — If a bro disrespects a woman, say something. Silence is a vote. "Not cool, man" costs you five seconds of awkwardness and may save her a lifetime of damage.

7:12 — Fight fair. No yelling to win, no threats, no using the past as a weapon. If you feel the anger rising, say "I need ten minutes," go for a walk, and come back. Never put hands on anyone, ever, in any argument. That is the line, and there are no exceptions.

7:13 — Compliment thy partner on something that is not her appearance this week. Her mind, her humour, her courage. Do it often. Do it without a motive.

7:14 — Learn to say "I'm sorry" without a "but." The apology with a "but" is a defence in disguise.

7:15 — Love is not a feeling alone. It is a daily decision made at 7 a.m. with bad breath and no feelings at all. The couples who last are the ones who keep choosing, not the ones who keep feeling.

7:16 — When a relationship ends, grieve it properly. Do not rebound into pain. Do not stalk. Do not text at 1 a.m. Call a brother, go for a run, and cry in the shower if needed. It will not last forever, it only feels like it.

7:17 — Not every woman is the same, just as not every man is. Do not let one bad experience turn into a belief about half of humanity. Bitterness is a poison thou drinkest hoping she will suffer.

7:18 — Whatever thy orientation, identity or relationship status, the same law holds: respect, honesty, consent and kindness. This law has no loopholes.
