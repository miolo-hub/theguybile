---
label: Chapter 3
title: The Laws of Food
---

3:1 — Whoever says "I'm not hungry" shall inevitably eat from another bro's plate.

3:2 — The last slice belongs to whoever has the courage to claim it.

3:3 — Never trust a bro who says "Let's order something light."

3:4 — He who cooks shall not wash. He who eats shall wash. He who only watches shall be assigned to the trash.

3:5 — Every man shall learn to cook five meals well. Not for women, not for show, but because a man who can feed himself is free. Takeout is a loan at high interest.

3:6 — Cereal is a legitimate dinner on three nights a week, and a cry for help on the fourth.

3:7 — Share the good fries. The fry-hoarder is known and remembered.

3:8 — Whoever grills is king for one afternoon. Do not question the king's char level. Eat the burnt thing and say "perfect."

3:9 — Drink water. Yes, you. The headache you blame on the universe was a glass of water away.

3:10 — A man who treats the waiter badly will treat his wife badly, his employees badly, and his friends badly. Tip well. The restaurant is a test, and you are always being graded.
