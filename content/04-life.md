---
label: Chapter 4
title: The Laws of Life
---

4:20 — Sleep is temporary, but scrolling is eternal.

4:1 — Nobody is watching you as closely as you think. This is the best news and the worst news you will ever receive. Go do the embarrassing thing.

4:2 — Comparison is a thief with a good Instagram. Everyone's highlight reel is cut from a Tuesday as boring as yours.

4:3 — Most problems are solved by sleep, food, water, a walk, or an apology. Try those five before you call it a crisis.

4:4 — The man who says "I don't have time" has time. He has a phone. Check the screen-time report and weep.

4:5 — Be on time. Being late says, "my time is worth more than yours," even if you meant, "traffic."

4:6 — Thou shalt learn to say "I don't know." It is the beginning of every skill and the end of every bluff.

4:7 — Thou shalt learn to say "I was wrong." It costs three seconds and buys ten years of trust.

4:8 — Fix one thing at a time. The man who tries to overhaul his whole life on January 1st is back on the couch by January 9th.

4:9 — You will outgrow some friends, some cities, and some dreams. Grieve them, thank them, and move on. This is called growing up, and nobody warned you it would feel like this.

4:10 — Do something kind that nobody will ever find out about. Your character is whatever you do when no one is watching, and the Wi-Fi is down.
