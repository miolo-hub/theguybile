### The Ten Commandments of the Guybile

1. Show up.
2. Tell the truth, kindly.
3. Listen before thou fixest.
4. Ask for help, and give it back.
5. Respect every woman, every man and every person.
6. Take care of thy body, mind and money.
7. Fail, learn, get up.
8. Check on the quiet one.
9. Never put hands on anyone in anger. No exceptions.
10. Text back.

### When to Call a Brother, and When to Call a Doctor

| Situation | Call a brother | Call a doctor or professional |
|---|---|---|
| Bad day, breakup, stressful week | Yes | Only if it lingers |
| Sad, empty or numb for two weeks or more | Yes, tell him | Yes, soon |
| Thoughts of ending thy life or harming thyself | Yes, right now | Yes, right now: a crisis line (988 in the US) or emergency services |
| Drinking, gambling or using more than thou intended | Yes, be honest | Yes, a doctor or counsellor |
| Chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden weakness or confusion | No, call emergency services | Emergency services immediately |
| A lump, a changing mole, blood where there should be none | Mention it to a friend | Yes, this week |
| Temper frightening thyself or thy family | Yes, tell him | Yes, a counsellor |
| Relationship stuck in the same fight | Yes, for perspective | Yes, couples counselling |
| Money panic, debt, job loss | Yes, for support | A financial adviser or debt-help service |
| Boredom, loneliness, wanting to chat | Yes, always | Not needed |

The Guybile is humour and brotherhood, not medical, legal or financial advice. When in doubt, ask a professional.
