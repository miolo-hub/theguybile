label: Chapter 29
title: The Final Verses and the Brother's Oath
---

29:1 — Be the man thy younger self needed.

29:2 — Strength is not how much thou canst take. It is how well thou canst protect, listen and carry on.

29:3 — Open thy mind. Read a book from the other side. Ask a question of a person who disagrees. Thou needest not change thy mind, but thou must be able to hear.

29:4 — Change the world by changing thy corner of it: the way thou speakest, the way thou treatest staff, the way thou raisest thy kids, the way thou showest up when it is boring.

29:5 — Laugh hard, especially at thyself. A man who can laugh at his own mistakes can survive nearly anything, and a man who cannot is easy to scare.

29:6 — Live so that at thy funeral the eulogy is easy, the story is long, and at least one person laughs so hard they have to leave the room.

29:7 — When in doubt, call a friend.

### The Brother's Oath

Read aloud at weddings, birthdays, graduations, and the third round:

*I will show up.*

*I will tell the truth, even when it is awkward.*

*I will listen more than I fix.*

*I will ask for help, and give it back.*

*I will respect every woman, every man and every person, in word, in deed and in the group chat.*

*I will take care of my body, my mind and my money.*

*I will fail, I will learn, and I will get up.*

*I will leave every room, and every friend, a little better than I found them.*

*And I will text back.*

**Amen. Or, as the brothers say: "Facts."**

*Add thy own verses. The Guybile is never finished. It is written by every man who lived it and told the story afterwards.*
