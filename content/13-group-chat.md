---
label: Chapter 13
title: The Laws of the Group Chat
---

13:1 — The group chat is sacred ground. What is said there is the true history of the friendship. Treat it with the respect it does not deserve.

13:2 — "Who's in?" with no date, place or time is not a plan. It is a rumour. Whoever asks shall also propose a Saturday.

13:3 — He who replies eleven days late with "lol sorry, crazy week" shall be forgiven the first time and teased every time thereafter.

13:4 — Voice notes longer than ninety seconds are a podcast nobody subscribed to. Send a text, or call.

13:5 — Muting the chat is allowed. Leaving the chat in a huff is a declaration of war. Mute first, leave never.

13:6 — Never screenshot the group chat and send it to anyone outside the group. This is the closest thing the Guybile has to a death penalty.

13:7 — When a brother posts bad news in the chat, the memes stop. Reply with something human. Then call him.

13:8 — A thumbs-up is not a conversation. Neither is "haha." Say a real sentence once a week.

13:9 — Every chat has the Silent One, the Meme Dealer, the Planner, the Chaos Agent and the Guy Who Reacts Only With Emojis. Know thy role, and love the others in theirs.

13:10 — Before sending a message at 1 a.m., ask: will I be proud of this at 9 a.m.? If the answer is "no," put the phone face down.

13:11 — When the chat has been quiet for months, be the one who breaks the silence. Somebody has to, and it might as well be thee.
