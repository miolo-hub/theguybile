---
label: Appendix B
title: The Glossary of the Guybile
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Ticket Man** | The man who promised to book it and did not. Speak his name with sighs. |
| **The Fry-Hoarder** | He who guards the shared fries. Known, remembered, forgiven slowly. |
| **The Silent One** | The friend who rarely speaks in the chat and carries the most. Check on him. |
| **The Meme Dealer** | Supplies the group with humour. Do not underestimate his emotional intelligence. |
| **The Planner** | Books the trip, holds the spreadsheet, is thanked too little. Thank him. |
| **The Chaos Agent** | Ensures no plan survives contact with the weekend. Also the source of the best stories. |
| **The 3 a.m. Brain** | The part of thy mind that insists everything is ruined. A liar with a microphone. |
| **Five Minutes Away** | An undefined unit of time, between 15 and 90 minutes. |
| **The Brother's Oath** | The nine lines at the end of the book, read aloud at weddings and the third round. |
| **Touching Grass** | Going outside to reset thy mind. Medically endorsed. |
| **The Bro Check** | A text or call to a friend that says, "how are you, really?" |
| **Man Up** | A phrase that has hurt millions of men. Replace with "talk to me." |
| **The Fourth-Floor Walk-Up** | The home of the friend who is moving. Thou shalt help him anyway. |
| **The Invisible Load** | The planning, remembering and organising that keeps a home running. Share it. |
| **The Last Slice** | The ultimate test of courage, manners and appetite. See Guybile 3:2. |
| **Mute, Never Leave** | The correct way to survive a noisy group chat. |
